PackBridge FAQ

Does PackBridge change Shopify orders?

No. PackBridge reads Shopify B2B orders, applies your pack-unit rules, and sends a downstream payload. It does not rewrite Shopify orders in the current release.

Is PackBridge an ERP?

No. PackBridge is a focused translation layer between Shopify B2B orders and the ERP, EDI, or WMS system you already operate.

How are webhooks signed?

Outbound payloads are signed with an HMAC signature using your PackBridge signing secret. Your receiving endpoint can verify the signature before accepting the payload.

What happens if delivery fails?

PackBridge records the failed delivery, keeps the job visible in the audit trail, and retries according to its delivery policy. You can inspect held or failed jobs from the app.
