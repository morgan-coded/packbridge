PackBridge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

PackBridge is a Shopify app that translates valid B2B orders into downstream-safe pack units (each → inner → case → pallet) for ERP, EDI, and warehouse systems. This policy explains what data PackBridge accesses, what it stores, and what it does with that data.

What PackBridge accesses

When you install PackBridge on your Shopify store, it requests the following read-only scopes:

  • read_orders — to process B2B orders as they are created
  • read_products — to mirror product and variant metadata for pack rules
  • read_companies — to resolve rules scoped to a B2B company
  • read_customers — required by Shopify to read the purchasing entity on a B2B order

PackBridge does not request any write scopes. It does not modify your shop data.

What PackBridge stores

  • Shop domain, plan, currency, and sync timestamps
  • Company and company-location identifiers and names (B2B only, no consumer PII)
  • Product and variant identifiers, SKUs, titles
  • Pack-rule configurations you create
  • Normalization jobs and per-line-item events — order identifiers, quantities, resolved rules, and delivery audit records
  • A per-shop signing secret used to sign outbound webhook payloads

What PackBridge does NOT store

  • Customer names, emails, phone numbers, or addresses
  • Payment information
  • Any personally identifiable information (PII) belonging to end customers

PackBridge deals strictly with company-level B2B identifiers. Consumer data is out of scope.

Where PackBridge sends data

When a B2B order is processed, PackBridge POSTs a signed JSON payload to a webhook destination URL that you configure in the app settings. Every request is signed with HMAC-SHA256 using your per-shop signing secret. If you do not configure a destination URL, no outbound delivery occurs.

PackBridge does not sell, share, or forward your data to any third parties beyond the webhook destination you configure.

Data retention

PackBridge retains pack-rule configurations and normalization audit logs for as long as the app is installed on your shop. When you uninstall PackBridge, your Shopify session is deleted immediately. Cached catalog and B2B metadata may persist for up to 30 days before being purged.

GDPR compliance

PackBridge subscribes to Shopify's mandatory compliance webhooks (customers/data_request, customers/redact, shop/redact). Because PackBridge stores no customer PII, these requests are processed as no-ops with a confirmation response.

Security

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database on a private network. Access is restricted to the PackBridge application via credentials not committed to source control. Webhook payloads are signed to allow you to verify authenticity on receipt.
